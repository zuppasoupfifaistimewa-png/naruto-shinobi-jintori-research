D. UK0 remaining work (not silently waived)
Still missing:
1. Full sequential scan of all EXE text and Entry 1 bytes for the exact pointer-slot/stride idiom, plus explicit list of hits and non-hits.
2. For each candidate, backward pointer provenance and forward instruction walk (maximum 24 instructions or until base overwrite).
3. A complete all-offset 0x00..0x123 load/store table, not just immediate-field filters.
4. Contiguous instruction windows for confirmed readers and writers at +0x00, +0x1A, +0x1C, +0x1E, +0x20.
5. Separate proven hits vs unresolved/non-matching candidates, without treating absence of an exact idiom as proof of no access.
The old census script does not implement those requirements. In this task scope, only report files may be added; old workflow/code may not be altered. The available GitHub tool integration has no workflow_dispatch, and there is no local ROM/EXE/DATA.BIN file available for a complete local re-scan. Therefore UK0 remains SEBAGIAN, not TUNTAS.
